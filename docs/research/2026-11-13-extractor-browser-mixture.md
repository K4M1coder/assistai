# EXP-169: Target-speaker extractor on the browser mixture

Hypothesis: the existing CPU VoxLock int8 extractor could remove the competing voice from EXP-168 while preserving the intended French speaker. The same 10.75-second synthetic mixture, including leading and trailing silence, was replayed in 20 ms chunks through two-thread VoxLock, then sent to local Vosk. This was an offline burst; enrollment, ASR and live device latency are excluded from the extraction timings. No physical microphone or new GPU model was used.

With a separate Microsoft Hortense enrollment, recognition returned `bonjour assistant puis veux-tu me raconter une petite anecdote sur la lune` in 9.766 seconds of extraction. With a separate Microsoft Zira enrollment, recognition returned `l'armement lourd assistant puis dire me raconter une petite barre sur la lune` in 9.347 seconds. The latter still contains much of the French target phrase, despite Zira being the competing voice. Vosk French recognition of English is a poor proxy for separation, so this does not quantify how much competing speech remains; nevertheless the negative control does not establish reliable target-speaker selection. Both runs occurred on an unisolated machine and processing time alone does not establish sustained real-time behavior.

An initial control used a Microsoft Paul enrollment even though the competing voice was Zira. It also returned French content and took 9.920 seconds, but it is an unmatched-enrollment control and must not be interpreted as selecting the competing voice. The corrected Zira enrollment was generated locally from a separate English sentence. Mixture SHA-256: `78e077b84fcf2f566d5007c64896fc56af994f677c9f68a10cd7f8bc26e85559`; Hortense enrollment: `600dbd865066cfda07e8a2fc63cea169e2b2126b33e5d685660b1aeb1e21e24e`; Zira enrollment: `6a93425875742b7ddb5f44f712f1551aee31ee51f53adaf885d5375114b817a0`.

The extractor remains experimental and is not enabled in the browser. Do not infer speaker identity, real-room separation, or safe voice interruption from this test. Reproduce with the existing VoxLock setup and the synthetic mixture and enrollments present using `.\.venv-voice\Scripts\python.exe scripts/probe-browser-mixture-extraction.py`.
